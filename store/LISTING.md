## Google Play

**App name** (≤30): PersianMeow: Learn Persian

**Short description** (≤80): Learn Persian (Farsi) A1–B2 with Finglish, audio for every word and a cute cat

**Category:** Education · **Tags:** Language learning, Education
**Contains ads:** No · **In-app purchases:** No (for now)

**Full description** (≤4000):

Learn Persian (Farsi) the friendly way: short lessons, real audio, and Pishi the Persian cat cheering you on.

No alphabet needed to start. Every Persian word and sentence also comes in Finglish, Persian in English letters (salâm = hello), with the vowels the script leaves out. You speak from day one and learn the 32 letters alongside.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 95 units from A1 (beginner) to B2 (upper intermediate)
• 4,600+ words, every one with its own recording and a slow button
• The Persian alphabet: all 32 letters, their joined shapes and six lessons
• Dialogues with 21 characters, each with a face and a matching voice
• Readings, grammar in plain words, idioms, fun facts and poetry
• Spaced review that brings words back just before you forget them
• A talking dictionary, searchable in English, Finglish or Persian

EXERCISES THAT STICK
Pick the meaning, match pairs, build sentences from tiles (right to left!), listen and choose, type the meaning, read Persian numbers, and answer questions on short texts.

STAY MOTIVATED
XP, a daily goal, hearts (or relaxed mode) and a streak on the Persian week, which starts on Saturday.

MADE TO BE RIGHT
Native Persian speakers review the pronunciation inside the app. Hear something odd? Tap "sounds wrong?" and it gets fixed.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no trackers. Use it without an account; sign in only if you want your progress on every device. Works offline.

Duolingo doesn't teach Persian. PersianMeow does.

### Data safety form (answers)

- Data collected: **Yes**, only for learners who create an account.
  - Personal info → Name, Email address: collected, not shared; purpose *App functionality, Account management*; optional (the app works without an account).
  - App activity → Other actions (lesson progress): collected, not shared; *App functionality*; optional.
  - User-generated content → Other (pronunciation reports): collected, not shared; *App functionality*; optional.
- Encrypted in transit: **Yes**. Users can request deletion: **Yes** (in-app and by email).
- No location, contacts, photos, audio recordings, device IDs, financial or health data.

### Content rating (IARC questionnaire)

Category *Reference, News or Educational*. No violence, sexuality, language, controlled substances, gambling. Users can interact: **No** (reports go to reviewers only, not to other users). Shares location: No. Expected rating: **Everyone / PEGI 3**.

### Target audience

13 and over (accounts need 16+ or a parent's permission). Not designed for children, so the Families policy doesn't apply.
